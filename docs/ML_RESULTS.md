## Limitations

Overlapping labels reduce effective sample size to about `n/30`; chains are correlated; there is one historical path; hyperparameters are fixed; the HAR baseline is an adaptation; thresholds are a priori; and results use one data snapshot at course scale. An LLM review is not an expert review.

The measured positive rate is 543/9,325 (5.82%) overall and 138/7,216 (1.91%) over the test union, far below the nominal 20%. The expanding Q80 is anchored by the very high volatility of 2017-2021; whether that explains the lower rates is a hypothesis and remains unverified.
